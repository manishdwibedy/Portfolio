Beyond the Prompt: A Deep Dive into RAG

Current Talk

This talk will explore Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) as a powerful solution to the limitations of traditional LLM prompting, such as hallucination and outdated knowledge. We will provide a deep dive into the RAG architecture, breaking down each component from data ingestion and chunking to vector embedding, retrieval, and generation. The session will go beyond basic concepts, exploring advanced retrieval techniques like hybrid and re-ranking search, and discuss practical strategies for optimizing performance. We'll conclude with real-world applications and a look at the future of RAG.
