Refund Policy

We strive to provide high-quality training and support. Please review our refund policy carefully.

Refund Eligibility

Refunds are only available if the course has not yet started or within the first 7 days of enrollment. After this period, no refunds will be issued.

How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please contact us at manish.dwibedy@gmail.com with your enrollment details and reason for the request.

Processing Time

Refund requests will be processed within 14 business days. Approved refunds will be returned using the original payment method.

Non-Refundable Items

Any additional materials or services purchased separately are non-refundable.
